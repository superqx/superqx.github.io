Hi, I'm Xin Qian, a researcher in Physics. I'm joining School of Physics and Shing-Tung Yau Center, Southeast University from Jan. 2026 as a postdoctoral researcher in Yunfeng Jiang's group. Before this, I earned my PhD degree in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Charlotte Fløe Kristjansen, from Niels Bohr institute (NBI), University of Copenhagen. Currently, I'm interested in applying the technique of integrability to the system out of equilibrium, Integrability provides exact results for the simulation of the system in non-equilibrium.
